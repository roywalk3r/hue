# Hue Streaming Platform: High-Fidelity UI Design Prompt

**Context & Vision:**
Create a premium, cinematic movie streaming platform named "Hue". The design should prioritize speed, immersion, and high-fidelity visuals. The target audience consists of movie enthusiasts who expect a modern, high-performance experience similar to premium services like Netflix or Apple TV+, but with a unique "Hue" branding.

**Design Philosophy (Stitch Mindset):**
- **Clarity over Prose:** Use structured, literal descriptions for elements.
- **Glassmorphism:** Emphasize the "Hue" theme with deep gradients and blurred UI layers.
- **Cinematic Vibe:** Use an obsidian-purple and neon-cyan color palette.

**Layout Structure (Desktop):**
1.  **Global Navigation (Navbar):**
    - Fixed at the top with a `backdrop-blur-2xl` glassmorphism effect.
    - **Logo:** A stylized play icon with a gradient background (Purple to Cyan) and the word "Hue".
    - **Links:** "Movies", "TV Shows", "Recently Added", and "My List" (Outfit font, semi-bold).
    - **Actions:** A sleek search icon and a primary CTA "Explore" button with a glowing hover state.
2.  **Immersive Hero Section:**
    - **Background:** A high-resolution, full-width backdrop of a trending movie (e.g., One Piece or Avatar).
    - **Overlays:** A subtle gradient fade to black at the bottom and right.
    - **Content:** Large, high-contrast title typography. A floating rating badge (Star icon + number). A 3-line synopsis.
    - **Buttons:** A primary "Watch Now" button with a neon-cyan glow and a secondary "More Info" button with a glass effect.
3.  **Content Discovery Rows (Carousels):**
    - Horizontal scrolling rows with section titles (e.g., "Trending Now", "Popular in your Region").
    - **Poster Cards:** Rounded corners (2xl), high-quality images, and a "scale-up" hover animation.
    - **Hover State:** Reveal metadata (Year, Rating, Genre) with a subtle bottom-up gradient overlay.

**Stylistic Directives:**
- **Typography:** Use 'Outfit' for titles and 'Inter' for UI labels.
- **Color Palette:**
    - Background: `#0a0a0f` (Obsidian)
    - Primary: `#a855f7` (Electric Purple)
    - Secondary: `#06b6d4` (Neon Cyan)
- **Effects:** Soft drop shadows on text, border glows on active elements, and 30px blur on glass panels.

**Prompt for Stitch Generation:**
"Design a cinematic desktop home page for a movie streaming site called 'Hue'. Use a deep obsidian theme with neon cyan and purple accents. Features a fixed blurred navbar, a massive hero section with a trending movie backdrop, and horizontally scrolling movie posters with rounded corners and scale-hover effects. The aesthetic should be high-end glassmorphism."
